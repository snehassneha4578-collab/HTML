HTML Entities Used

1. &lt;        - Displays the Less Than symbol (<)
2. &gt;        - Displays the Greater Than symbol (>)
3. &amp;       - Displays the Ampersand symbol (&)
4. &copy;      - Displays the Copyright symbol (©)
5. &trade;     - Displays the Trademark symbol (™)
6. &reg;       - Displays the Registered symbol (®)
7. &#8377;     - Displays the Indian Rupee symbol (₹)
8. &#128512;   - Displays the Emoji (😀)
9. &nbsp;      - Inserts a non-breaking (extra) space
